# Frame Scores as Situation Impact Meters

The MVP will keep Relationship, Stress, and Performance as visible meters because they make consequences legible during a short demo. They will be presented as estimated effects on the current situation, not as measures of the player's character or ability; there will be no leaderboard or overall worth score.

This keeps the useful feedback loop while reducing the risk that a learner interprets a poor attempt as a judgment about themselves.
