# Position as an Educational Training Tool

Signal Shift will be presented as an educational practice tool, not as diagnosis, therapy, or a guarantee of employment outcomes. Before public release, at least one autistic lived-experience reviewer and one employment-support practitioner should review the scenarios and coaching, with particular attention to Red Light content.

This boundary protects the learner from overpromising and gives safety-sensitive content an appropriate review path without expanding the MVP into a clinical programme.
