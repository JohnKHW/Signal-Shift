# Define the Joke-to-Red Boundary

Joke Signs will be limited to low-risk, non-literal remarks where the tone and context support a light response. A remark that targets identity or vulnerability, causes meaningful discomfort, repeats after a boundary, or escalates into disrespect will be treated as a Red Light requiring a Safety Response.

This prevents the game from teaching the learner to dismiss harmful conduct as humour and makes the boundary between social flexibility and self-protection explicit.
