# Allow Multiple Acceptable Responses

Signal Shift will distinguish a scenario's preferred response from other acceptable responses instead of treating every non-preferred choice as wrong. This better reflects real workplace variation, preserves player agency, and reduces the risk of teaching rigid social rules; coaching will explain trade-offs and consequences rather than label the player.

**Considered Options**

- One unique correct response per scenario.
- Multiple acceptable responses with one preferred response. **Chosen.**

**Consequences**

Scenario data must support response validity and nuanced coaching, and scoring must reward quality on a range rather than only matching one answer.
