# Add an Unseen Transfer Check

Signal Shift will include a short, unseen scenario after the taught scenarios to test transfer of the Classify → Confirm → Respond method. This gives the demo observable evidence of learning beyond recall while keeping the MVP within its short-session constraint.

**Considered Options**

- End after the fixed scripted scenarios.
- Add a full fifth level.
- Add a short unseen Transfer Check. **Chosen.**

**Consequences**

The Transfer Check needs its own content, scoring, and feedback treatment, and it must not reveal the signal category before classification.
