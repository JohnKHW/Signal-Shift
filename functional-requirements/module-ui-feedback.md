# Module: UI & Feedback

## FR-UI-001
The main play screen SHALL contain these elements at minimum:
- Scene background (simple office or meeting room)
- Character name + dialogue text box
- Signal Evidence area (dialogue, context, and observable behaviour)
- Classification buttons (5 signal types)
- Signal Reveal area that remains hidden until classification
- Response option buttons (exactly 3)
- Three Impact Meters (Relationship / Stress / Performance)
- Coaching feedback panel that appears after choice

## FR-UI-002
Signal icons MUST be large enough and high-contrast for easy recognition under time pressure.

## FR-UI-003
Before classification, the five classification controls SHALL show each Signal Type's name and distinct icon without revealing the scenario's answer. After submission, the Primary Signal Type SHALL be highlighted, incorrect choices SHALL be dimmed, Signal Reveal and classification coaching SHALL appear, and response options SHALL become active.

## FR-UI-004
Classification coaching SHALL appear immediately after classification. Response coaching and qualitative impact feedback SHALL appear within 0.5 seconds of the final choice and remain visible until the player advances.

## FR-UI-005
A clear “Next” or “Replay” control SHALL be present after coaching is shown.

## FR-UI-006
The game SHALL be fully playable on desktop browsers (primary target for Cloud Studio demo). Mobile responsiveness is P2.

## FR-UI-007
The Signal Reveal SHALL use the fixed visual representation for the selected Primary Signal Type, but no answer icon or signal name SHALL be shown in the scene before classification.

## FR-UI-008
Impact Meters SHALL display direction and broad magnitude (for example, improved, stable, or worsened significantly). Precise numeric deltas SHALL not be shown to the player.

## FR-UI-009
The home screen SHALL offer Start Learning and Demo Mode. Demo Mode SHALL reach a representative decision without forcing the player through the tutorial first.
