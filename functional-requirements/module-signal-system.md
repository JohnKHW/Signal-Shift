# Module: Signal System

## FR-SIGNAL-001
The game SHALL define exactly five player-facing Signal Types with fixed visual representations and meanings:

- Green Light: Clear, executable instruction
- Yellow Light: Ambiguous suggestion, probe, or incomplete expectation that calls for clarification or observation
- Red Light: Warning or escalating conflict risk that calls for pausing, de-escalation, boundary-setting, or support
- Detour Sign: Sudden change of plan or priority
- Joke Sign: Low-risk, non-literal humorous remark where a light response is appropriate

Classification SHALL be action-oriented: it SHALL be based on the useful or protective next action supported by the available evidence, not on guessing the speaker's private intention.

## FR-SIGNAL-002
Before classification, every workplace scenario SHALL display Signal Evidence only: dialogue, tone, behaviour, context, or timing. It SHALL NOT display the Primary Signal Type's name or answer icon.

## FR-SIGNAL-003
Every decision SHALL have exactly one Primary Signal Type. A scenario MAY include Supporting Cues that point toward another interpretation, but the player SHALL not multi-select signal types. Coaching SHALL explain why the Primary Signal Type best determines the next action.

## FR-SIGNAL-004
After the player classifies a signal, the system SHALL immediately reveal the Primary Signal Type and a short coaching explanation of the action it supports. The reveal SHALL occur before response options become active.

## FR-SIGNAL-005
Signal classification is a mandatory first step before the player is allowed to choose a response action.

## FR-SIGNAL-006
The MVP SHALL include at least one active Red Light scenario, delivered as the unseen Transfer Check. It SHALL teach pausing, protecting a boundary, de-escalating, or seeking appropriate support.

## FR-SIGNAL-007
A Joke Sign SHALL be limited to a low-risk, non-literal remark. If a remark targets identity or vulnerability, causes meaningful discomfort, repeats after a boundary, or escalates into disrespect, it SHALL be treated as a Red Light scenario requiring a Safety Response.
