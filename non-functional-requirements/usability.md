# Non-Functional Requirements — Usability

## NFR-USE-001
Character dialogue SHALL use natural Hong Kong Cantonese. Instructions, controls, and coaching SHALL use clear, direct Hong Kong Traditional Chinese. English toggle is P2. Avoid idioms, sarcasm, and figurative language in instructional text.

## NFR-USE-002
Critical interactive elements (signal buttons, response buttons) SHALL have minimum touch/click target size of 44×44 px.

## NFR-USE-003
Colour alone SHALL NOT be the only means of conveying signal type. Each signal MUST also have a distinct shape or icon + text label.

## NFR-USE-004
The player SHALL never be left without a clear next action. Empty or ambiguous states are forbidden.

## NFR-USE-005
Coaching language SHALL be supportive and non-judgemental (for example, “呢個情況比較像黃燈，因為…” rather than “你錯了”). It SHALL distinguish preferred, acceptable, and harmful trade-offs.

## NFR-USE-006
The UI SHALL not display precise Impact Meter deltas such as `+15` or `-10`. It SHALL use understandable direction and broad magnitude such as improved, stable, or worsened significantly.

## NFR-USE-007
The player SHALL be able to understand what to do next at every state: observe Signal Evidence, classify one Primary Signal Type, choose a response, read feedback, or advance.
