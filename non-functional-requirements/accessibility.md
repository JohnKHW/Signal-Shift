# Non-Functional Requirements — Accessibility (Neurodiversity)

## NFR-ACC-001
The interface SHALL avoid auto-playing sound or sudden large motion by default.

## NFR-ACC-002
Focus order SHALL be logical (dialogue and Signal Evidence → classification → Signal Reveal and coaching → responses → Impact feedback → advance).

## NFR-ACC-003
All interactive elements SHALL be operable by keyboard alone.

## NFR-ACC-004
Text contrast SHALL meet WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 for normal text).

## NFR-ACC-005
Players SHALL be able to pause or take unlimited time on any decision; no hard timers that force failure.

## NFR-ACC-006
Signal Type SHALL never be conveyed by colour alone. Each classification control and Signal Reveal SHALL include a distinct icon, shape, and text label.

## NFR-ACC-007
The interface SHALL respect reduced-motion preferences and SHALL not use sudden large motion, flashing, or auto-playing sound to communicate consequences.
