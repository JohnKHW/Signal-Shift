# Non-Functional Requirements — Performance

## NFR-PERF-001
Initial page load (including all assets needed for Demo Mode and the first Start Learning level) SHALL complete in under 3 seconds on a typical Hong Kong broadband connection.

## NFR-PERF-002
Scene transitions and feedback appearance SHALL feel instantaneous (< 300 ms perceived delay).

## NFR-PERF-003
The entire MVP SHALL remain a single-page application with no full page reloads during a play session.
