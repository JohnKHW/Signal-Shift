# Signal Shift Domain

This glossary defines the learning concepts and player-facing language for Signal Shift. It keeps the game's educational promise precise as the scenarios and interactions evolve.

## Audience

**Primary Learner**:
An autistic young person aged roughly 16–25 who is preparing for work or is newly employed and wants practical support with ambiguous workplace communication.
_Avoid_: High-functioning autistic youth, socially deficient user, special-needs player

**Training Tool**:
An educational practice experience inspired by real workplace situations; it is not a diagnostic, therapeutic, or employment-guarantee product.
_Avoid_: Therapy, treatment, clinical intervention, employability fix

**Measurement Mode**:
An explicitly opt-in future mode that records pseudonymous session data for longitudinal research or treatment evaluation, separate from ordinary gameplay.
_Avoid_: Hidden tracking, default analytics, clinical score mode

**Game Impact Data**:
The in-session classification, response, and Impact Meter changes used for immediate coaching; these are gameplay signals and are not clinical outcome measures.
_Avoid_: Treatment outcome, diagnostic score, efficacy proof

**Lived-Experience Review**:
Review of scenarios and feedback by autistic people who can assess whether the experience is respectful, realistic, and useful.
_Avoid_: User testing only, expert-only review

**Language Split**:
Character dialogue uses natural Hong Kong Cantonese; instructions, controls, and coaching use plain, direct Hong Kong Traditional Chinese with no idioms, sarcasm, or unnecessary figurative language.
_Avoid_: Formal translated dialogue, ambiguous instructional prose, English-first MVP

## Learning Model

**Core Learning Outcome**:
When a player encounters an ambiguous workplace signal, they classify it, confirm what is meant, and then choose a measured response.
_Avoid_: Emotion recognition, social skills training, guessing the right answer

**Acceptable Response**:
A response that is safe, plausible, and moves the workplace situation forward, even when it is not the scenario's preferred response.
_Avoid_: Correct answer, perfect response, one right way

**Preferred Response**:
The response the scenario recommends because it most directly confirms the practical meaning and supports the player's stated goal.
_Avoid_: Only correct answer, model behaviour

**Transfer Check**:
A short unseen scenario at the end of a session that tests whether the player can apply Classify → Confirm → Respond without having been taught the exact script beforehand.
_Avoid_: Final exam, bonus level, memory test

**Replay Variant**:
A semantically equivalent scenario with changed wording, roles, or surface details that tests the same decision principle without requiring cross-session accounts or persistence.
_Avoid_: Random noise, harder mode, a new lesson

**Demo Mode**:
A short entry path that places a new player directly into a representative decision scenario, then shows Signal Reveal, coaching, and a concise summary without requiring the full tutorial first.
_Avoid_: Separate game, presentation-only mockup, skip-learning mode

**Demo Slice**:
The 60–90 second portion of the experience used to demonstrate the core decision loop; it is not the complete learning path.
_Avoid_: Full session, complete curriculum, final assessment

**Impact Meter**:
A visual estimate of how the player's chosen response may affect the current situation across relationship, stress, and performance; it is not a measure of the player's character, ability, or worth.
_Avoid_: Player score, personality score, leaderboard rating

**Qualitative Impact Feedback**:
The player-facing description of an Impact Meter change using direction and broad magnitude, such as relationship improved or stress rose significantly, rather than precise numeric deltas.
_Avoid_: Clinical measurement, exact probability, scientific score

**Pattern Summary**:
A session-end explanation of the player's learned signal types, recurring confusion, Transfer Check result, and next practice suggestion; it is not a single overall grade.
_Avoid_: Final grade, pass/fail result, total score

**Recoverable Consequence**:
A visible but non-terminal change in the current situation that lets the player learn from a choice and continue playing without a Game Over or forced restart.
_Avoid_: Punishment, failure state, social penalty

**Outcome Priority**:
When Impact Meters conflict, the game prioritises safety and wellbeing, then clarity and agency, then relationship, then short-term performance.
_Avoid_: Optimise manager approval, productivity at any cost, social compliance

**Classify → Confirm → Respond**:
The canonical three-part action sequence taught by the game: identify the signal category, ask or observe until the practical meaning is clear, then act accordingly.
_Avoid_: Classify → Respond, react first, decode emotions

**Workplace Signal**:
A piece of workplace dialogue or behaviour whose practical meaning affects what the player should do next.
_Avoid_: Emotion, mood, social cue

**Signal Evidence**:
The observable dialogue, tone, behaviour, context, or timing shown before classification; it gives the player enough information to reason without naming or displaying the answer.
_Avoid_: Hint icon, signal label, answer cue

**Signal Reveal**:
The signal icon, name, and explanation shown after classification so the player can compare their interpretation with the intended lesson.
_Avoid_: Prompt, pre-classification hint, answer button

**Action-Oriented Classification**:
Classifying a signal by the next useful or protective action supported by the available evidence, rather than by guessing the other person's private intention.
_Avoid_: Mind reading, intention detection, reading emotions correctly

**Confirmation Response**:
A player response that turns an unclear or changed situation into an actionable understanding by asking for the missing goal, priority, boundary, timing, or support needed.
_Avoid_: Clarifying question as a separate game phase, interrogation, asking everything

**Primary Signal Type**:
The single Signal Type that best determines the player's next useful or protective action in a scenario.
_Avoid_: All signals present, multi-select answer, objectively only signal

**Supporting Cue**:
Additional dialogue, tone, behaviour, or context that modifies how the Primary Signal Type should be understood without becoming a second classification answer.
_Avoid_: Secondary answer, hidden trick, extra signal type

## Signal Types

**Signal Type**:
One of the five player-facing categories used to describe a Workplace Signal: Green Light, Yellow Light, Red Light, Detour Sign, or Joke Sign.
_Avoid_: Signal colour as the only label, social cue type

**Yellow Light**:
An ambiguous suggestion, probe, or incomplete expectation that requires clarification or observation before acting.
_Avoid_: Bad feedback, negative signal, uncertainty

**Red Light**:
A warning or escalating conflict risk where the player's first responsibility is to pause, reduce escalation, and seek appropriate support when needed.
_Avoid_: Refusal, angry signal, automatic compliance

**Safety Response**:
A measured response to a Red Light that protects the player's boundary or wellbeing by pausing, naming the immediate issue, and involving a trusted person or formal support path when appropriate.
_Avoid_: Defiance, confrontation, people-pleasing

**Detour Sign**:
A sudden change to the plan or priority that requires confirming the new goal, order of work, or deadline.
_Avoid_: Change request, interruption, surprise

**Joke Sign**:
A non-literal humorous remark where a light, non-defensive response is more appropriate than a literal or hostile interpretation.
_Avoid_: Sarcasm, teasing, insult

**Joke-to-Red Boundary**:
A Joke Sign stops being a low-risk joke when it targets a person's identity or vulnerability, causes meaningful discomfort, repeats after a boundary, or escalates into disrespect; those cases require a Red Light/Safety Response.
_Avoid_: All teasing is harmless, just take a joke, always respond lightly
